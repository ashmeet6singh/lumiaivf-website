# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Women undergoing IVF treatment — from initial consultation through embryo transfer. Emotionally invested, often anxious, navigating a complex multi-week protocol involving daily medications, frequent scans, blood tests, and emotional highs and lows. European audiences are a primary focus (EN + PL from day one). May want to share data with a partner or doctor. Phone-first users; the site must feel as warm on iPhone as on desktop.

## Product Purpose

Lumia is a warm, empathetic mobile web app for women going through IVF treatment. The marketing site establishes Lumia's public presence, communicates the product's warmth and clinical utility, and collects email sign-ups from interested users so they can be notified at launch. Success means visitors feel understood — not processed — and trust the product enough to join the waitlist.

## Brand Personality

Warm, compassionate, unhurried. Speaks like a friend who also happens to understand follicle counts. Celebrates the act of tracking and self-care, not just outcomes. Never clinical, never alarming, never pushy.

Three words: warm, clear, companionate.

## Anti-references

- Cold clinical health apps (Clue, Ovia pre-redesign) — data-first, emotionally flat
- Generic fertility trackers that don't understand IVF protocol phases
- SaaS landing pages: feature grids, metric counters, glassmorphism, hero-metric templates
- Loud wellness brands: aggressive gradients, loud CTAs, "transform your journey" copywriting
- Editorial-typographic AI-generated pages: display serif + italic + broadsheet grid (not the right lane for a companion app)

## Design Principles

1. **Speak to the whole person.** Clinical accuracy never at the expense of emotional warmth.
2. **Earn the next scroll.** One dominant idea per fold; don't rush the visitor.
3. **Warmth is carried by voice and detail, not decoration.** No gratuitous animation or ornament; every element earns its presence.
4. **Mobile-first, always.** The user is on her phone, often tired, often anxious — design for that context first.
5. **Calm confidence.** The design should feel like a steady hand, not a sales pitch.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Reduced motion support required (Framer Motion `useReducedMotion`). All interactive elements keyboard-navigable. Color is never the sole conveyor of information. Polish (PL) and English (EN) parity for all UI copy.
